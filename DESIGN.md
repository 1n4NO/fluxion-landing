## Palette

- Paper ground: warm off-white `#f4f0e8` with near-white content surfaces.
- Signal: orange `#f36d2f` for primary action, emphasis, and active workflow state.
- Atmosphere: sea-glass teal `#94c0c0` and deep teal `#315d62` for supporting surfaces and status.
- Ink: `#252625` for high-contrast copy and controls.

## Type and composition

- Display type uses Space Grotesk with tight tracking and oversized, short headlines.
- Body copy uses DM Sans with a calm, readable measure.
- Sections alternate between paper, teal, and orange fields; do not collapse the page back into a uniform card grid.
- Product proof is shown as diagrams, screens, and terminal output rather than decorative icon tiles.

## Interaction

- Primary actions are pill-shaped, high-contrast, and use a small diagonal arrow.
- The quickstart uses real tabs with visible active state and keeps code legible on small screens.
- Motion is restrained to the marquee and small control transitions; content remains visible without animation.
